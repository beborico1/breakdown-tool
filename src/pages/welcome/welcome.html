What the colors mean

    Join words into a phrase

    Click a colored word and it turns into a band. Click the word next to it and the two join. Keep going and hover the band to see the whole phrase translated. Clicking a word that is not touching the band starts again there, and Esc clears it.

    On a headline or anything else that is also a link, a plain click still belongs to the page. Hold Alt and click to join those words instead.

    Turn it on

    Coloring works on every site once you switch it on. Chrome will ask to let this extension read data on all sites — that is how it finds Japanese on the page you are reading.

    You can pause it on any individual site from the toolbar popup, or switch it off entirely at any time.

    Keep the words you meet

    Every word you hover is saved to Your words, where you can review what you have seen and send a selection to Anki. Cards go to Anki running on your own machine.

    There is also Speak & check: say something in Japanese and see it broken down. That one uses Chrome's speech service, so your microphone audio goes to Google while it is listening.
